**Ridge Runner — Alpine Courier** is an original 3D flight game that runs entirely offline in a desktop browser. Fly a low-poly courier plane over a procedural alpine valley — lake, village, pine forest and a 420 m runway — with a lit HUD (speed, altitude, heading, attitude indicator, throttle, fuel and damage gauges). Bank to turn, manage throttle and fuel, thread glowing ring gates, and bring the plane home: landings are scored on softness, speed and alignment, hard touchdowns damage the airframe, and flying into terrain ends the flight.

Three missions: **Ring Circuit** (8 rings, beat the clock), **Runway Landing** (take off, fly 3 guide rings, land on runway 09), and **Canyon Dash** (10 rings on 55% fuel, timed). Keyboard and mouse controls, chase and cockpit cameras, synthesised WebAudio engine and wind (no audio files), pause, and a persistent best score.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```

No build step, no network requests: `game/index.html` plus its local modules and a vendored `three.min.js`. Controls: **← →** bank & turn, **↑ ↓** climb / dive, **Shift / Ctrl** or **E / Q** throttle, drag the mouse to steer, **C** camera, **P** pause, **M** sound, **1 / 2 / 3** pick a mission.
